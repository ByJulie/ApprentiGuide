# System card: ApprentiGuide

- **Purpose and users:** answers the questions of aivancity apprentices, students looking for an apprenticeship and international students on their contract, pay, leave, working time, absences and exams, meal vouchers, health plan and right to work, in French or English, citing the exact article, Service-Public.fr fact sheet or school rule, with its version date. When the answer depends on the employer or the school, it gives the general rule and whom to ask and what. **Information, not legal advice.**
- **Use policy:** for general information on the rules, to prepare a question to HR, the school or the prefecture. Not for: legal advice or a decision in a dispute (prud'hommes, dismissal); immigration decisions (it does not replace the prefecture); school calendars, fees, a given company's salaries or offers; homework, CVs, anything outside the rights of apprentices and students. Do not type personal data (it is masked, but not all of it: names and dates of birth are not). Check every answer against its source before acting on it.
- **Models:** answer gpt-oss-120b on Groq (open weights, Apache 2.0, temperature 0, prompt `prompts/system_v3.txt`); local fallback Qwen3 8B through Ollama (nothing leaves the laptop). Rewrite of the question in French: gpt-oss-120b. Embeddings bge-m3 and reranker bge-reranker-v2-m3, speech-to-text faster-whisper `medium`, all local. Evaluation judge qwen/qwen3.8-27b (not the answer model).
- **Data:** Code du travail (794 articles in scope), CESEDA (35), Code de la sécurité sociale (14) through the Legifrance API (PISTE); 64 Service-Public.fr fact sheets (DILA open data, Licence Ouverte); aivancity study rules and internal rules 2025-2026 (73 chunks, used with the school's permission, not published). 1507 chunks, texts of 25 and 30 Oct. 2026. No personal data in the corpus.
- **How it works:** input guardrail; follow-up questions made complete and rewritten in French; three retrieval signals (article number, PostgreSQL full text, bge-m3) fused, reranked, 5 passages from 5 documents; abstention in code (low reranker score, or a named article not in the corpus) with a fixed message; answer citing every fact; sources, links and version dates built from the database; a fixed sentence towards the school's service for international students when a residence or work-authorisation text is cited.
- **Evaluation** (`docs/evaluation_report.md`): 99 questions; correct and cited, judged by an LLM calibrated on 40 human labels (kappa 0.74) plus citation checked in code: **0.56** (95 % CI 0.45 to 0.67) with prompt v3, 0.44 with v2 (official run), against 0.01 for the same model without documents; NOT FOUND 18/19 against 0/19; no invented citation in v3. Target 0.8 not reached; v3 was written after reading the failures, so its score is optimistic.
- **Safety:** guardrail in code (email, phone, social security number and IBAN masked before any cloud call; fixed refusal for prompt injection; help lines 3114 and 15 for distress); abstention in code; only links from the database are shown (links and images written by the model are not displayed); uncited ids flagged; nothing logged, audio kept in memory. Red team: 28 attacks, 23 passed, 2 safe but not ideal, 3 failed (`docs/red_team_log.md`).
- **Limitations:** snapshot of the law (an article can change); collective agreements not covered; general rule, not the person's own case; an ambiguous question can be answered with a guess; some rules are badly retrieved (maximum working time, trial period); English answers come from French texts; small test set used during development, no hold-out set.
