# System card: ApprentiGuide

- **Purpose and intended users:** answers the questions of aivancity apprentices, students looking for an apprenticeship and international students about their apprenticeship contract, pay, leave, working time, meal vouchers, health plan and right to work, in French or English, with the exact article or Service-Public.fr fact sheet it comes from. When the answer depends on the employer or the school, it gives the general rule and what to ask HR or the school. It gives **information, not legal advice**.
- **Out-of-scope uses:** legal advice or a decision in a dispute (prud'hommes, dismissal); immigration decisions (it does not replace the prefecture); school calendars and fees (only the aivancity regulations 2025-2026 are in the corpus: the full study rules and articles 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 and 17 of the internal rules); a given company's salaries or offers; homework, CVs, anything unrelated to the rights of apprentices.
- **Models:** answer: gpt-oss-120b on Groq (open weights, Apache 2.0, reasoning effort low, temperature 0); local fallback Qwen3 8B through Ollama (nothing leaves the laptop). Rewrite of the question in French: gpt-oss-120b. Embeddings bge-m3 and reranker bge-reranker-v2-m3 (local). Speech-to-text faster-whisper `small` (local). Evaluation judge: qwen/qwen3.8-27b on Groq (not the answer model).
- **Data:** Code du travail (794 articles in scope), CESEDA (35) and Code de la sécurité sociale (14) through the Legifrance API (PISTE), and 64 Service-Public.fr fact sheets (53 Particuliers, 11 Entreprendre) (DILA open data, Licence Ouverte), and the aivancity regulations 2025-2026 (73 chunks, used with the school's permission), 1507 chunks; files of 25 and 30 Oct. 2026, version date shown under every answer. No personal data in the corpus. User questions are not stored; personal data in a question (email, phone, social security number, IBAN) is masked before any cloud call.
- **How it works:** (1) input guardrail; (2) follow-up questions made complete, question rewritten in French; (3) three retrieval signals (article number, PostgreSQL full text, bge-m3) fused, reranked, 5 passages from 5 different documents; (4) abstention in code when the best score is too low or a named article is not in the corpus; (5) answer citing every fact, sources shown from the database with their link and date.
- **Evaluation summary:** see `docs/evaluation_report.md`. Main metric, correct and cited (LLM judge calibrated on human labels, kappa 0.74 on 40 answers, plus citation checked in code): **TBD** (95 % CI **TBD**) against **TBD** for the same model without documents; NOT FOUND on questions without an answer: **TBD**; invented citations: **TBD**. Known failure types: company-dependent questions answered NOT FOUND, a related rule given instead of the exact one asked.
- **Safety measures:** input guardrail (personal data masked, fixed refusal for prompt injection, help lines 3114 and 15 for distress); abstention decided in code with a fixed message; sources and links built from the database, uncited ids flagged; prompt forbidding general knowledge; no logging of questions. Red-team: 28 attacks, results in `docs/red_team_log.md` (**TBD**).
- **Known limitations:** snapshot of the law at the end of Oct. 2026 (an article can change); collective agreements not covered, and of the school regulations only the parts listed above; the answer can be faithful to a text and still not fit a particular situation; questions about the person's own situation are answered with the general rule, not their case; English answers are translated from French texts; the evaluation set is small (81 questions, intervals of about ±10 points) and the abstention threshold was set on it.
